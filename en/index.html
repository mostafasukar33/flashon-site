Stamped test reports from two government laboratories

Flash On conduit was tested in May 2025 at the Energy Research Center, Faculty of Engineering, Cairo University, and at the Housing and Building National Research Center. The Energy Research Center report concludes that the sample technically conforms to Egyptian Standard 1283/2008.

Energy Research Center results – fire-resistant conduit, 20 mm and 25 mm
Dimensions20 mm: outer diameter 20.2 mm, inner 16.1 mm. 25 mm: outer 25.1 mm, inner 20.5 mm.
CompressionNo cracks or deformation.
Impact12 samples, none shattered.
Bending and heatConforms in both tests.
Dielectric strength2000 V, 50 Hz, 5 minutes: no insulation breakdown.
Insulation resistanceAbove 1 GΩ for all samples.
Flame resistanceSelf-extinguishing in under 10 seconds.
ConclusionTechnically conforms to Egyptian Standard 1283/2008.
